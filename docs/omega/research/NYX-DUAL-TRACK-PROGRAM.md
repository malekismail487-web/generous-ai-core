## Additive goal text

### Demonstrated path — unchanged objective

Continue the existing evidence-backed benchmark and capability program. Diagnose failures by class, reproduce general defects on separate development tasks, implement the smallest coherent correction, and evaluate transfer on fresh tasks using unchanged acceptance checks and defensible compute-matched controls. Preserve original first-attempt, repaired, negative, incomplete and blocked outcomes separately. Seek at least 80% on the agreed accessible benchmarks; do not substitute confidence, selected failures, ever-correct accumulation, infrastructure checks, or a different benchmark protocol for observed results. Public benchmark exposure and untested populations remain explicit uncertainties.

### Theoretical goals — additive research objective

Investigate how NYX can invent task-appropriate tools, acquire reusable verified procedures, compose symbolic and model reasoning, design discriminating experiments, and propose improvements to its own reasoning workflows and improvement methods. Build a broad, research-grounded hypothesis portfolio, including unconventional combinations, without representing plausible architecture as demonstrated intelligence. Select hypotheses by observed bottleneck, leverage, information gain, cost and risk. Test one bounded intervention at a time against a simpler matched control; require fresh cross-domain transfer and independent admission before promoting any capability claim.

For controlled recursive-improvement research, compare whether an improved version is better both at solving new tasks and at producing a subsequent improvement. Preserve versioned lineage, clean-environment reproduction, regression checks, rollback and human oversight. Candidate systems must not control their authoritative scoring, reserved evaluation data, evidence custody, authority kernel or promotion decision. Improvements to detectors may be proposed but must be independently validated and admitted under a separate reviewed version. No unrestricted self-modification, hidden permission growth, autonomous production deployment, account access or physical actuation is authorized by this amendment.

Alternate the tracks at completed, cohesive milestones. A theoretical proposal returns to the demonstrated path for empirical testing; a demonstrated failure informs the next theoretical question. Do not run an unbounded autonomous experiment scheduler, interrupt a frozen evaluation to redesign it, or expand architecture merely to fill time. Preserve negative evidence. Record AGI/ASI as an aspiration, not a result implied by benchmark scores, self-editing, entity count, tool libraries or downloaded neural maps.

## Research findings and their limits

These are primary-source-informed design inferences for NYX, not replications or claims of novelty. Abstract-only inspections are identified; they are not full-paper methodological reviews. Before implementing an intervention, inspect the relevant full methods/code and licensing rather than treating this initial review as exhaustive.

The research does not establish that any combination guarantees AGI, ASI, all-benchmark success or independence from model capability. NYX still uses Nemotron for model-backed cognition. ## Alternation and promotion

1. Finish the current demonstrated-path diagnosis/correction milestone, including adversarial regression. Do not change its frozen past results.
2. Select one theoretical hypothesis with a concrete job exposed by those results. First candidates are H005 (task-specific tool synthesis), H009 (procedure distillation), H045 (bounded self-improvement proposal) and H048 (two-generation recursion measurement), in that dependency order—not all at once.
3. Reproduce its failure mode on separate development tasks; freeze intervention, limits, simpler control, source identity and acceptance criteria before fresh transfer.
4. Execute only in an already authorized boundary. Report blocked dependencies without substituting synthetic evidence for operational capability.
5. Return to the demonstrated path: paired fresh outcomes, safety regression, quality, calibration, realized compute, false acceptance, unknown usage and failure class.
6. Promote only the scoped benefit supported by the evidence; otherwise revise, defer or retire the hypothesis. Preserve the rejected candidate and reasoning summary. A changed evaluator requires separately versioned validation, never retroactive regrading of frozen results.

Alternation occurs at milestone boundaries; it is not an automatic 50/50 scheduler. No theoretical mechanism receives preference just because it is more elaborate. Reliability/supporting work and capability improvements remain separate. Broad gains require unrelated tasks and replication, not repeated success on exposed failures.

For any empirical selection over many proposals, account for multiple testing and selection bias. Keep a development corpus, an intervention-selection validation corpus, and an untouched final transfer corpus. Once inspected for diagnosis, a task is exposed and must not be represented as fresh. Reserve final evaluation for a frozen candidate, report uncertainty and regressions, and predeclare stopping rules. All search, critic, repair and tool compute counts toward the comparison.

The candidate can propose an experiment or evaluator; it cannot certify itself. ## First falsifiable research sequence

**Immediate:** finish source-site emission hardening and independent disposable integration tests. Then compare whole-source repair against source-site-enabled repair from the same passing-but-quality-rejected starting candidates. Use fresh tasks, frozen policy, unchanged model, calls, tokens, execution limits, original-state quality comparison, and separate first-attempt/repair outcomes. Preservation of untouched syntax is not preservation of semantics; the full oracle still decides. No causal gain is claimed before that comparison.

**Next eligible theoretical prototype:** NYX encounters a development objective requiring evidence it cannot obtain from an existing tool. It proposes one small pure transformation/checker with typed inputs/outputs, preconditions, resource bound and failure modes. A trusted independent harness checks its behavior, adversarial cases and safety. A separately admitted executable artifact can then be reused on fresh objectives through existing Omega tool requests. Compare against a no-new-tool control under total matched resources, charging tool construction and validation as well as inference. This does not grant automatic tool registration or arbitrary host execution.

**Later recursive experiment:** freeze parent P; let P propose a bounded workflow/tool candidate C in disposable isolation; independently evaluate C; let P and C each propose a next candidate under equal construction/evaluation budgets; test all final candidates on a never-inspected transfer set. A better C is first-order improvement. C producing better subsequent candidates is the stronger recursion claim. Failing the second condition must not be renamed recursion.
